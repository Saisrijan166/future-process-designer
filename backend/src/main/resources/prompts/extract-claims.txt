TASK:
Extract up to {{max_claims}} claims from the text above that are relevant to the research goal.

A claim has two parts:
- "claim": one self-contained assertion in your own words, understandable without the source open.
- "quote": the words from the SOURCE TEXT that support it, COPIED EXACTLY, character for character.

The quote requirement is absolute and it is checked mechanically. After you answer, every quote is
searched for in the source text above. A quote that is not found there is discarded and the claim
is marked unverified. Do not paraphrase inside the quote. Do not fix the source's typos, spelling,
capitalisation or spacing. Do not join two separate sentences into one quote. Do not add ellipses.
Copy 15 to 60 unbroken words from the text.

If the source says nothing relevant to the research goal, return an empty list. That is a correct
and useful answer. Never manufacture a claim to fill space, and never quote something the source
did not write.

Choose "claim_type" from exactly these values:
- STATISTIC   a measured number or proportion
- REGULATION  a legal or regulatory requirement
- CAPABILITY  something a technology can or cannot do
- RISK        a documented failure mode, harm or hazard
- PRACTICE    how the work is done in practice
- BENCHMARK   a comparison against a baseline or a human
- DEFINITION  what a term means
- OPINION     someone's judgement rather than a finding

Where the claim carries a figure, also fill "numeric_value" (a plain number, no commas or symbols)
and "numeric_unit" (for example "percent", "hours", "INR", "days"). Where the source states what
period or year the claim applies to, fill "as_of" as YYYY-MM-DD or YYYY. Leave any of these out if
the source does not state them; do not estimate.

Return STRICT JSON ONLY, no prose, no markdown fences, in exactly this shape:
{
  "claims": [

      "claim": "one assertion",
      "quote": "exact words copied from the source text",
