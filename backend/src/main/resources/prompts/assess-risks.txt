TASK:
Produce the risk register for this redesign. Return between 5 and 12 risks.

Categories, use exactly these values:
PRIVACY, BIAS, ACCURACY, SECURITY, COMPLIANCE, OPERATIONAL, CHANGE, VENDOR, TRANSPARENCY

Rules:
1. Every risk must be specific to THIS process and THIS design. "The model may hallucinate" is not
   a risk entry; "a fabricated citation in a generated feedback comment reaches a candidate and is
   quoted in an appeal" is.
2. Cover the categories that genuinely apply. A design that processes candidate data and does not
   have a PRIVACY entry has not been reviewed. A design that makes or influences a decision about a
   person and has no BIAS entry has not been reviewed either.
3. "mitigation" must be a control someone could implement and audit — a threshold, a review gate, a
   retention limit, a logged override, a published notice. Not "ensure quality" or "monitor
   closely".
4. "owner_role" names who owns the control. Use a role from the process where one fits.
5. "obligation" is for a specific legal or standards requirement, where the evidence above
   establishes one. Name the instrument and what it requires, and cite it as [n] in the description.
   If the evidence does not establish an obligation, leave "obligation" empty rather than asserting
   one from memory — a fabricated legal requirement is worse than a missing one.
6. "cited_evidence" lists the evidence numbers supporting the risk. Cite only numbers shown above.
7. likelihood and impact are 1 to 5. Impact 5 means a candidate is harmed, a regulator becomes
   involved, or results have to be withdrawn.
8. Where the risk belongs to one intervention, copy its exact description text into "opportunity".
   Leave it empty for a risk that spans the design.
