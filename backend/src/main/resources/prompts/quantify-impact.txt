You are estimating four inputs. The application computes hours and money from them, so give the
inputs and not the conclusions:
- volume_per_month: how many items pass through this step in a month
- minutes_per_item: how long one item takes a person today, end to end
- automation_share: the fraction of that time the intervention genuinely removes, 0 to 1.

- hourly_cost_inr: fully-loaded hourly cost in Indian rupees of the person doing this work today

Rules:
