Rules for the future process:
1. It must be a complete process, not a list of improvements. Somebody must be able to follow it
   from the first step to the last. Include steps that do not change.
2. Every step states what the human is accountable for and what the AI does. A step where AI does
   the work end to end is AI_AUTOMATED; where AI drafts, ranks or flags and a person decides it is
   AI_AUGMENTED; where AI has no meaningful role it is HUMAN_LED. Do not label a step AI_AUTOMATED
   if a person is still checking every output — that is AI_AUGMENTED.
3. "failure_mode" is required for every step that involves AI: what happens when the model is
   wrong or unavailable, and how the process keeps running. A step with no answer to that is not
   designed yet.
4. "handoff_note" says how work passes between the person and the system at this step: what the
   person sees, what they can override, what gets logged.
5. "replaces_activity" names the current activity this step replaces, copied EXACTLY from the
   CURRENT ACTIVITIES list, or an empty string for a genuinely new step.
6. Where the change affects how long the step takes, say so in "cycle_time_note", in plain terms.
7. Do not eliminate a step that produces a required record, an approval or an audit trail. Redesign
   who does it, not whether it happens.

Part two — "ai_interventions": one entry for every future step that differs from today, explaining
the change.
8. "future_activity_name" must match a name from part one EXACTLY.
9. "related_ai_opportunity_description" must copy the EXACT description text of the opportunity that
   justifies the change, so the two can be linked. Leave it empty only for a change that no
   opportunity above motivated.
10. intervention_type: NEW for a step with no equivalent today, ELIMINATE for work that disappears,
    AUTOMATE when AI takes the step over, AUGMENT when AI assists a person.
